Answers based on Legal Metrology Rules, 2011

Today

Does this imported packaged food product comply with the principal display panel requirements for MRP declaration? The sticker seems to cover the original text.

Based on the visual evidence and regulations, covering original declarations with a sticker for MRP is subject to specific conditions.

Affixing individual stickers on the package for altering or making declaration is not permissible. However, for reducing the Maximum Retail Price (MRP), a sticker with the revised lower MRP (inclusive of all taxes) may be affixed, provided that the original MRP remains legible.

gavel Source: Rule 6(1)(a) & Rule 18(2), Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011.

What if the original price was 500 INR and the sticker says 600 INR?

This constitutes a direct violation. No manufacturer or packer or importer shall alter the price on the wrapper once printed and used for packaging.

Increasing the MRP via a sticker is strictly prohibited. Stickers can only be used to declare a price reduction.

Violation Detected

gavel Source: Rule 18(2) & 18(3), Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011.
